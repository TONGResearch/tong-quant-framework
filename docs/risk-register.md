# Engineering Risk Register

| ID | Severity | Risk | Mitigation | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Critical | Survivorship or future-data leakage creates false validation results. | Strict `available_at`, replay warnings, trust matrix, adjusted-bar rejection. | Historical provider coverage remains incomplete. |
| R-002 | High | Process crash strands notification work or a stale worker overwrites a reclaimed attempt. | Expiring leases recover orphans; acknowledgement matches the current attempt, channel, and recipient. | A recovered send may duplicate an externally accepted message; exactly-once external delivery is not guaranteed. |
| R-003 | High | Credentials enter persistent notification data. | Model, repository, and SQLite-entry validation reject credential-like assignments; errors store type names only. | Unlabelled opaque secrets cannot be detected reliably; access controls remain necessary. |
| R-004 | High | Partial multi-table writes corrupt a Research or Validation run. | Atomic final-run transaction boundary. | Older persisted partial runs need separate audit and cleanup policy. |
| R-005 | High | Database is marked current after an incomplete schema change. | Ordered migrations, checksums, migration-head metadata, verified backups, and isolated restore audits. | Downgrade, free-space preflight, and broader post-migration domain invariants remain pending. |
| R-006 | High | Orphaned analytical child rows create inconsistent reports. | New dead-letter relation uses an FK; repositories preserve ownership. | Existing tables require staged FK migrations. |
| R-007 | Medium | AKShare, direct/transported CNInfo, or Yahoo endpoint/column drift changes normalized evidence. | Raw hashes, schema validation, fake-client tests, bounded direct access, twice-weekly monitoring, owner-assigned health reports, repeated-condition escalation, and defect-only CI failure. | The observation horizon is still short and monitoring cannot by itself prove historical continuity. |
| R-008 | Medium | Notification channels leak provider errors or configuration. | Environment-only credentials and safe error codes. | Third-party channel behavior still requires sandbox verification. |
| R-009 | Medium | Research risk heuristics are mistaken for portfolio authorization. | Artifact naming, disclaimers, execution guards, proposal-only contracts. | Human interpretation and governance remain necessary. |
| R-010 | Critical | Future trading functionality bypasses validation or risk. | Execution disabled and guarded; no Broker, Order, Fill, or Paper Trading implementation in this phase. | Must be re-audited before any trading-related milestone. |
| R-011 | High | A multi-step provider ingestion leaves normalized data without its batch, fingerprint, or warning audit chain. | Domain and audit writes now share one SQLite transaction; failure-injection regression test added. | Failed attempts are not yet recorded in a separate post-rollback failure ledger. |
| R-012 | High | Credential diagnostics become persistent provider fingerprints. | Capability reports retain only configuration status; token length and masked prefix were removed. | Historical copies outside the repository may still exist and should not be redistributed. |
| R-013 | Medium | Schema created from `SCHEMA` diverges from schema reached through migrations. | Immutable baseline registration, physical fingerprint checks, fresh/legacy convergence tests, and verified restore drills. | Downgrade and broader post-migration domain invariant checks remain incomplete. |
| R-014 | Medium | Provider-specific ingestion coupling makes future adapters bypass common audit or PIT controls. | Provider-neutral capability/evidence contracts and one transactional ingestion service. | Legacy AKShare jobs still use the older typed pipeline and require gradual migration. |
| R-015 | Critical | Mechanically valid replay is mistaken for trustworthy historical evidence. | ReplayConfidence, PITReadiness and visible warnings remain mandatory. | Core datasets are still `UNSUITABLE`; historical claims and live use remain blocked while controlled-launch engineering proceeds in parallel. |
| R-016 | High | A healthy live contract probe is mistaken for trusted or replay-ready data. | Monitor reports explicitly state that health cannot upgrade trust or PIT readiness. | Governance and longitudinal calibration are still required before any promotion. |
| R-017 | High | A future developer or low-context AI follows stale chat or milestone text and crosses a frozen boundary. | Canonical project status, machine-readable governance, bounded work packages, CI audit, and PR checklist. | Semantic contradictions outside checked markers still require review. |
| R-018 | High | Scheduled provider reports disappear or cannot establish historical continuity. | Immutable baseline manifests, retained input references, twice-weekly history cache, uniquely named 90-day artifacts, credential-free content-addressed evidence bundles, embedded-history identity checks, and no-overwrite archive/verification plus isolated restore-drill commands are implemented on pinned supported CI runtimes. Both the private local and AES-256 encrypted iCloud copies of the October 8 artifact passed independent restore drills on October 10. The first provenance-qualified October 12 observation also has two independently verified copies with matching audit hashes and table counts; CloudDocs confirmed the encrypted copy caught up on October 13. | Representative multi-period history, recurring encrypted rotation, and future-batch restore exercises remain incomplete. |
| R-019 | Medium | Database corruption or a failed migration cannot be recovered. | Read-only audits, SQLite online backups, no-overwrite restore drills, corruption/interruption tests, and an operator runbook are implemented. | Scheduling, encrypted off-device replication, free-space alerts, and real incident exercises remain manual. |
| R-020 | High | SQLite artifact metadata survives while its external filing bytes are missing, changed, or omitted from backup. | Content-addressed paths, SHA-256 verification on write/read collision, mode `0600`, immutable capture history, and transaction-failure cleanup. | Artifact-directory backup, inventory audit, and co-restoration with SQLite remain operator responsibilities. |
| R-021 | High | Cross-provider bar agreement is mistaken for provider trust or PIT-safe history. | Explicit unadjusted basis, trust ceilings, unavailable/incompatible attempt states, input hashes, and `LOW/RETRIEVAL_TIME` defaults for Yahoo/Massive. | Real correction, delisting, identifier, action, and continuity evidence has not accumulated. |
| R-022 | High | Missing, stale, or short-horizon provider evidence is mistaken for observed poor quality or silently omitted from readiness review. | Content-addressed evidence inventory, explicit artifact status, unknown-value preservation, cause classification, immutable effective classification, and a fail-closed 84-day/eight-check audit for every required scope. The October 12 and 15 scheduled observations were independently matched to Actions events and artifact contents. | Each required scope has only 2/8 qualified scheduled checks and 2.9/84 qualified days. The October 15 artifact is verified but not yet durably replicated; expected limitation-driven degradation remains visible. |
| R-023 | High | Correlated price indicators are counted as independent confirmations, creating false confidence and overfitting. | Group technical evidence by family, cap each family contribution, preserve conflicts, and require ablation plus out-of-sample validation. | Family definitions and validation thresholds are not implemented; `WP-RESEARCH-001` remains planned. |
| R-024 | Critical | A size strategy reconstructed from surviving securities, current classifications, or current market caps creates survivorship and look-ahead bias. | Require point-in-time market cap, historical eligible universe, lifecycle state, liquidity, and corporate-action evidence before size/style research. | Required evidence is not ready, so size/style conclusions remain blocked. |
| R-025 | High | Startup-point or dynamic-stop research uses future follow-through or assumes fills that were unavailable during a limit, suspension, or gap. | Timestamp setup-state transitions and keep stop levels as research invalidation references until a future market-rule-aware simulator and broker reconciliation exist. | Paper Trading, broker behavior, and execution are intentionally unimplemented. |
| R-026 | Critical | A mechanically complete replay is validated despite undeclared, low-trust, missing, or non-PIT-ready source evidence. | Explicit readiness datasets, minimum trust, as-of readiness hashes, incomplete-sample preservation, and a fail-closed ValidationRequest gate. | Dataset declarations are caller-owned and external sources can still contain undetected errors; review and cross-provider evidence remain mandatory. |
| R-027 | Critical | Date-insensitive quantity, sellability, or price-limit assumptions produce false historical fills or unsafe live checks across A-share and global markets. | Context-free helpers fail closed; the limited SSE pilot verifies pinned official references and binds ordinary quantity, confirmed settlement, odd-lot, and daily-band calculations to scoped evidence. A one-snapshot runtime inventory preserves closed dates, exceptional statuses and action revisions without inventing trust or action-free flags. | Trusted calendar/price provenance, phase/action coverage and account-lot readers plus intraday/cost constraints and simulation integration remain incomplete. The default operational database was absent at the October 16 local inspection. Neither source recovery nor an input inventory authorizes simulation. |
| R-028 | Critical | Placeholder readiness identifiers or a ValidationReport ID are mistaken for verified authorization or accepted validation. | Portfolio writes verify persisted lineage; a read-side gate checks status, age, versions, and configuration at the decision time. A settings-backed policy constructor rejects the default unpinned Validation hash. Execution remains disabled. | No reviewed Validation hash is pinned yet, the future simulation boundary does not require this check, and PIT eligibility plus any future short-lived live approval remain separate. |
| R-029 | High | A numeric horizon pass is mistaken for eight scheduled observations, or empty calibration/conflict ledgers are mistaken for provider agreement. | Schema `0.8.1` records new run origins; the versioned horizon auditor counts only recorded scheduled runs, treats old runs as `unknown`, and preserves legacy artifact verification. PIT readiness remains `UNSUITABLE`. | GitHub run references are self-recorded and require independent event verification; the scheduled monitor still does not populate calibration/conflict ledgers. |
| R-030 | Critical | Pressure to launch quickly causes Paper Trading or a broker sandbox to be mistaken for authorization to use real capital. | Evidence and launch engineering now run in parallel, but live execution remains disabled; Paper, sandbox, and live identities are separate work packages with explicit gates. | Human pressure can still bypass documented process; micro-live requires a separate future approval and hard capital limits. |
| R-031 | Critical | Portfolio or execution consumes an identifier without verifying the authoritative Validation, Portfolio, and Risk record behind it. | Typed references are verified on write and rechecked together in one read snapshot; stale, conditional, mismatched, and tampered lineage fails closed. | Pre-lineage proposals need explicit treatment; the future simulation boundary must bind exact artifact hashes plus PIT, market-rule, and independent risk controls. |
| R-032 | High | Wrong or substituted exposure classifications hide concentration. | Candidate classifications now reach Risk unchanged; configuration factories and model-version checks prevent silent interpretation drift. | Classifications remain caller-declared and need PIT provenance; weighted scalar correlation and drawdown proxies remain uncalibrated. |
| R-033 | High | A later ingestion silently changes the historical bar or calendar used by research. | Same-version bar/calendar writes are now immutable and idempotent; conflicts require a new available_at version with PIT regression tests. | Prior overwrites are not recoverable from code; security-master same-version correction provenance remains incomplete. |
| R-034 | High | Missing/partially normalized corporate actions or duplicated observations are mistaken for no risk or cumulative adjustments. | Supported descriptions now corroborate units, numeric components, provider implementation labels and ex-dates; real 26-row samples produce 33 events. Row rejections, exact-key idempotence and conflicts are audited, and the CLI returns partial/exit 2 for incomplete acceptance. | Provider labels are not issuer verification; unsupported formats, authoritative coverage, legacy units and independent continuity remain unresolved. All new observations are LOW/RETRIEVAL_TIME; adjustment and no-action inference remain disabled. |
| R-035 | Critical | An assumed historical availability time, matched disclosure date or fabricated fallback turnover contaminates research. | New AKShare market/fact ingestion uses retrieval-time visibility; numeric facts remain LOW/RETRIEVAL_TIME, optional turnover is null, cache parameters are versioned and real sample corrections retain separate versions. | Legacy backdated facts/bars/calendar and fabricated-zero rows are not repaired by the code upgrade. Raw-evidence audit/reconstruction and provider calibration remain mandatory; PIT is UNSUITABLE. |
